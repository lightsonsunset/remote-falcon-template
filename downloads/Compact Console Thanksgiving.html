Can I choose a song?

Yes. Open the Music tab during show hours. In Jukebox mode, picks join the queue. On voting nights, the song with the most votes plays next.

Why can I not choose a particular song?

Some songs take a break to give other favourites a turn. The message under a song tells you when it will be available again.

How do you build a show like this?

Planning, props, wiring, controllers, sequencing, testing and a suspicious number of zip ties all come together before opening night.
