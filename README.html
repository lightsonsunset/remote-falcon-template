Lights on Sunset template

Your show. Your colours. Your way.

A ready-made Remote Falcon viewer template, with Christmas and Halloween themes. Personalise its colours, wording and optional features using the visual customiser, or download the template and edit the HTML yourself.

By christmasdave · Lights on Sunset. The customiser is specifically for this template and its existing layout.

Open the template customiser · Download template + instructions · Full setup guide

Choose from three logo designs per theme, including pixel-light displays, with an optional editable name heading. A Home-name switch supports uploaded logos with lettering. Seven navbar decorations are available across Christmas and Halloween. Menu labels can be hidden for icons-only navigation, and the On air dot has an optional gentle glow. The header’s What’s new button shows recent updates.

Get started in six steps

1. Add your show details

Choose Set up my show on the welcome screen for a short wizard: Your show, Dates & hours, Listening, then Review & finish. Pick a theme, enter your name and optional logo, add show hours and choose FM, PulseMesh or both. Everything remains editable afterwards. You can also enter these details directly under Site setup.

The welcome also offers Open the editor and Take a quick tour. Closing it or pressing Escape starts editing. Your choice is remembered in this browser. Reopen the wizard using Setup wizard in the header; it starts with your current settings. Back keeps your answers, Skip leaves that step as it was when you entered it, and Cancel setup applies nothing. Only Apply & open editor saves the draft. Your other settings stay intact; unedited defaults follow your chosen theme. These are page details, not FPP scheduling or PulseMesh activation.

Replay the six-stop orientation using Quick tour in the header. The tour keeps your settings and returns you to where you started.

2. Personalise the pages

AreaWhat you can change
HomeWelcome, visitor guidance, notices, extra information, video link and countdown
MusicNEW sashes, category filters, queue wording, optional counters and playlists
SupportSeparate cards for charities, creators and thank-yous, with optional images and Home/Info links
InfoIntroduction, YouTube, Quick Facts, FAQs and useful resources
Song messagesRequest and voting titles and short explanations, with local preview buttons

Selecting a page opens it in the preview. Shared show details, colours, schedule and listening options live in Site setup. Start with the features you need; optional extras can stay off.

3. Check the preview

Try Mobile, Tablet or Desktop, then On air / Off air and Jukebox / Voting. The viewport choices check layout, not a particular phone's operating system. Mouse dragging scrolls phone/tablet previews; the wheel also works.

The preview is a labelled demonstration with no connection to your show. Trying songs or messages does not send requests or votes to RF. Its mode switches are preview-only; RF controls the real viewer.

4. Bring your song list (optional)

Use Import RF song CSV to preview your own titles, artists and artwork. Detect categories sets up filter buttons so visitors can browse groups of songs. Check that their names and order match RF; this does not change the categories saved in RF. Set NEW sashes lets you choose new songs without typing their exact sequence names. Manual entry is also available under Music.

Your CSV stays in this browser. The export uses RF's live catalogue, and your settings backup does not contain the imported CSV. Keep the original file. Import and category instructions.

5. Keep a settings backup

The customiser saves your edits locally in this browser. Use Download settings backup to keep a portable copy, and Load settings backup to continue later or on another computer. Clearing browser data can remove local edits. To move from a local preview or another website address, download a settings backup there and load it here. Import your song CSV separately.

6. Copy your page into RF

Choose Copy HTML or Download my HTML. In RF, create a separate draft under Viewer Page → New Page, replace its HTML with the complete document, then save and check RF's validator and preview. Test your own show before choosing Set as Live.

Both music modes and availability states are included in the export. No custom JavaScript enters your RF page. Real requests, voting, availability, countdown values and playback are supplied by Remote Falcon.

Prefer to edit the HTML yourself?

Download the ready-made package, unzip it, and open START-HERE.html. It includes editable Christmas and Halloween HTML files, images, instructions, credits and the licence. You do not need to use the customiser.

The guide covers NEW sashes, Home notices, support cards, image hosting and other optional features.
