    Automatically include your show details

    In editable headings and messages, use {show} for your show name, {charity} for the donation recipient, {season} for your selected theme’s name, and {frequency} for your FM number.

    For example, Welcome to {show} stays up to date when you rename your show. You can also just type your own text. Reset wording restores that field’s theme default.

          Import details & help

          Import a Remote Falcon sequence CSV to see your own names, titles, artists and artwork. The file stays in this browser; the exported viewer still uses RF’s live catalogue.

          Export from RF’s Sequences page. Maximum 2 MB / 2,000 songs.

          What the import includes

          CSV files do not include RF’s saved category order, visibility, NEW status or current limits/votes. Confirm the filter order against RF. Imported song clicks only simulate requests here. Save settings files do not include the catalogue; keep your original CSV.
